# Minimum Order Quantities

| | |
|---|---|
| **Document** | MOQ-01 — Minimum Order Quantities |
| **Version** | 2.0 |
| **Effective** | 1 January 2026 |
| **Owner** | Production Planning, Finland |
| **Applies to** | All sales orders and order lines |

## 1. Scope and principles

1.1 Minimum order quantities (MOQ) are set per **product** and are applied per **order line**, not per order. An order with several lines must meet the MOQ on every line separately.

1.2 Quantities must be a whole multiple of the increment in section 2. A quantity that is not a multiple is rounded **up** to the next increment.

1.3 The MOQ applies to the combined quantity of the same SKU on one order, whatever the quantities are per delivery (see DEL-01, section 4.3).

## 2. MOQ and increments by category

| Product category | Unit | MOQ | Increment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corrugated Box | pcs | 500 | 100 |
| Folding Carton | pcs | 1,000 | 500 |
| Paper Sack | pcs | 2,500 | 500 |
| Liquid Packaging Board | reel | 2 | 1 |
| Custom-printed products (any category) | pcs | 25,000 | 1,000 |

2.1 Custom-printed products always use the custom-printed row in the table, whatever their base category.

2.2 Reels are counted in whole reels; a partial reel is not a valid order quantity.

## 3. Minimum order value

3.1 The minimum net value of an order is **€250**, excluding freight and excluding credit note offsets.

3.2 An order below €250 net is combined with the customer's next order unless the customer accepts the €180 handling charge (DEL-01, section 5.3 applies in addition).

## 4. Below-MOQ orders: exceptions

4.1 A line below the MOQ is refused as a standard order.

4.2 Two exceptions exist, and nothing else:

| Exception | Limit | Frequency | Fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sample / trial order | up to 200 pcs, or 1 reel | once per customer per 12 months | none |
| Spare / replacement order | up to 50% of the MOQ | once per customer per calendar year | €250 handling |

4.3 A sample order is always marked as a sample, is non-returnable, and cannot be converted into a production order at the sample price.

4.4 A custom-printed product has **no** below-MOQ exception: sample orders are not available for custom print.

## 5. MOQ and delivery date changes

5.1 The company cannot reduce a line below its MOQ. A request to shrink an order to a quantity below the MOQ is refused, and the customer is offered a cancellation and a new order instead.

5.2 The assistant has no tool that changes quantities, prices or order lines: quantity changes are outside the change request process (CHG-01, section 1.4) and must be referred to customer service.

## 6. What the assistant must do

6.1 When a customer asks whether a quantity is possible, compare it with the MOQ and increment for that product category and answer with the exact values, for example "the MOQ for Paper Sack is 2,500 pcs in increments of 500".

6.2 If the requested quantity is below the MOQ, name the exception in 4.2 that applies, or explain that no exception applies.

6.3 Never accept a below-MOQ quantity by promising a later adjustment, and never invent an MOQ that is not in the table in section 2.
