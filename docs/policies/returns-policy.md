# Returns Policy

| | |
|---|---|
| **Document** | RET-01 — Returns and Claims Policy |
| **Version** | 2.1 |
| **Effective** | 1 January 2026 |
| **Owner** | Quality Manager, Finland |
| **Applies to** | All sales orders, business-to-business only |

## 1. Scope

1.1 All sales are business-to-business. The EU consumer right of withdrawal (14 days) does **not** apply, and the company never issues cash refunds.

1.2 Returns are settled with a credit note against a future invoice. Credits are approved by a supervisor; the assistant never promises one.

## 2. Return window and conditions

2.1 Goods may be returned within **30 calendar days** of the delivered date, counted from the date on the delivery note.

2.2 A return is accepted only when all of the following hold:

- the goods are unused and in their original, unopened packaging,
- the delivery note number and the product SKU are quoted,
- the original pallet or bundle labels are intact,
- the return is authorised in advance (section 5).

2.3 Returns of standard (non-custom) products after 30 calendar days are refused. The only exception is a hidden quality defect (section 4.3).

## 3. Non-returnable goods

3.1 The following can never be returned, with no exceptions:

- custom-printed or customer-branded products,
- opened Liquid Packaging Board reels, or reels with fewer than the ordered count remaining,
- food-contact items whose protective wrapping has been opened,
- made-to-order sizes produced specifically for one order,
- goods the customer has stored outside the conditions on the delivery note.

3.2 Liquid Packaging Board reels must be inspected at delivery. A reel damage claim must be noted on the carrier's delivery note, otherwise the claim is refused.

## 4. Quality claims and transport damage

4.1 Visible transport damage must be reported within **5 working days** of delivery, with photographs of the pallet, the label and the damage.

4.2 A quality claim must quote the delivery note number, the product SKU and, for reels, the reel number.

4.3 A hidden defect (a fault that only appears during processing) may be claimed for up to **12 months** after delivery. Claims after that period are refused.

4.4 For food-packaging products a claim must also state the batch code; without it the claim cannot be processed.

## 5. Return authorisation and freight

5.1 Every return needs a return authorisation number (RMA), issued by customer service within **2 working days** of a complete claim.

5.2 Return freight is paid by the company when the cause is our production, picking or transport error.

5.3 Return freight is paid by the customer for all other returns, including ordering errors on the customer side.

5.4 Goods returned without an RMA number are refused at the gate.

## 6. Fees and credits

6.1 Non-defective returns carry a restocking fee of **15%** of the net value of the returned lines.

6.2 The minimum value of a return is **€250 net**. Smaller quantities may be kept free of charge, but no credit is issued.

6.3 Defective goods are replaced or credited **without** a restocking fee.

6.4 The credit note is issued within **10 working days** of the goods arriving back at the warehouse.

6.5 A credit note is valid for **12 months** and can be offset against any invoice.

## 7. What the assistant must do

7.1 The assistant cannot register a return: there is no return tool, and it must not claim that a return, replacement or credit is already agreed.

7.2 It may explain the window (30 calendar days), the reporting deadline for transport damage (5 working days), the restocking fee (15%), the minimum return value (€250) and the credit timing (10 working days), quoting the relevant section.

7.3 When a claim is inside the allowed window, the assistant collects the delivery note number, SKU and reason, and tells the customer that customer service will issue an RMA within 2 working days.
